## The finding that matters

Three data points now separate the two candidate causes:

- Tue 25: 320 m of descent in short stair laps → next-morning 1/10, trivial.
- Fri 28: 569 m of sustained trail descent → **3/10 at rest**.
- Sat 29: 11.7 km at 5:40/km, HR 132, **39 m of descent** → 0.2/10 next
  morning, no flare at all.

Saturday was an unauthorised session, and it accidentally ran the cleanest
experiment of the block: high run volume at a brisk pace with the descent
removed did not touch the knee. **Sustained eccentric descent is the driver.
Run volume and pace are not.** That is worth more than the session cost.
