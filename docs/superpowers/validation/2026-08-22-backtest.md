## 4. Judgment

- **The expected catch happened:** the coach's week stays under 9 h and has
  no unplanned 4.5 h ride, so the Excessive spike (1.57–1.64) would not have
  occurred. Invariant 1 (hours cap) plus the planned-big-day rule are doing
  their job.
- **The Saturday motivation collapse is a real signal** the coach structure
  addresses: two ~11 h weeks with no down week preceded it. The 3–4 week
  mesocycle with a mandatory down week exists precisely for this.
- **Against over-tightening:** the athlete tolerated the isolated big day
  extremely well (felt great, no heat issues). The rules already allow
  planned big days absorbed by a down week — this is the right amount of
  permissiveness. No tightening.
- **Too timid?** Historical weeks ran 10–12 h vs the plan's ≤9 h — but the
  9 h cap is the athlete's stated availability, not a fitness ceiling. Not a
  miscalibration. (Separately, the athlete has since ruled that optional
  mobility runs may overshoot the cap — already encoded in coach-rules.)

## 5. Verdict

**Calibrated.** No rule changes required from this backtest. One athlete
trait recorded in athlete.md: tolerates isolated long endurance days well
when fresh enough — plan big days confidently, but only planned ones.
