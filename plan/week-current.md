# Week 2026-W37 — Divca sharpening, build week 3 → Bukovački maraton

run is the **Bukovački maraton, 35.7 km / 980 m D+**, run as a hike-run at
training effort. It replaces the planned FG 15 km / 400 m session AND the
Tuesday stair repeats — both dropped because the week's descent ledger has
room for exactly one thing, and it is the race.

**Why yes:** it is a full rehearsal of the Divca execution rule (walk the
descents from the start, climb by HR) at two-thirds of Divca's descent, with
a 16-day taper behind it to absorb the day. Your profile tolerates isolated
planned big days followed by a down block. **Why it is not free:** 980 m of
descent in one session is +54 % on the largest dose the knee has taken
(635 m → 0.7/10) and +72 % on the dose that flared it (569 m → 3/10). The
mitigation is not optional: **every descent is walked, from km 0.**

09-04/05, ≤1 on 09-06) → no descent progression: weekly run descent stays at
the 1,017 m baseline (W36 actual). Strength → **stage 1, load only, no
plyometric additions** (rule 2026-09-05), gated on Monday's cold reading
≤1/10. No stairs. Desk-heavy Mon–Fri → stretching B on weekdays, movement
breaks every ~60 min.

## Saturday — 2026-09-12 — BUKOVAČKI MARATON 35.7 km / 980 m D+ (LONG RUN, HARD) — 23.3 °C max, 15.5 °C at 06:00, 24 % rain

  climb is not a race.
- **Descent rule: EVERY descent is walked, from km 0.** Not "steep pitches"
  — every downhill. Cadence-short, slightly wider steps, land soft, no
  braking strides. Today's model is the 09-06 hike (376 m walked → ≤1/10),
  not the 09-04 run. Runners will pass you on the downhills; let them.
  Expected finish ~4:30–4:50.
- **Abort rule (stands, no exceptions):** lateral tightness → walk it out
  for 5 min. Not settled → **stop at the next aid station and get a lift**.
  A DNF costs nothing; a 3/10 flare 16 days before Divca costs the taper.
- **Fueling — 9 items, one every 30 min from 0:30:** 3 Alesto fruit bars
  (57.6 g), 2 Balans bars (59 g), 2 SiS gels (50 g), 2 × 30 g Haribo
  portions (30.8 g) ≈ **197 g carbs, ~42 g/h**. Fruit bars with water
  sips. Nothing untested from aid stations except water. Order: gel early
  on the first climb, bars through the middle, Haribo on the last hour
  when chewing gets hard.
- **Fluids:** ~500 ml/h at 23 °C — carry 1–1.5 L soft flasks, refill water at
  every aid station, **1 electrolyte tab per litre** across the day.
- After: 25–40 g protein within the hour, then a real meal. Legs up.
- **Knee reading Saturday evening → log/itb.md.** The Sunday and Monday cold
  readings decide taper week 1.
- Calories: ~4600 kcal (2235 + race ~2200 + ride to the start ~150) —
  carb emphasis, eat until you're not hungry, then eat once more.
- Stretching: A, short and gentle, post-race; no deep ITB stretching into
