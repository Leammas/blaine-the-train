# Macrocycle: August 2026 → Summer 2027

Benchmark on record: **Divca 2025 (Divčibare, 2025-09-27): 27.97 km, 2:55:09,
avg HR 153, 6:16/km.** The 2026 goal is a time ≤ 2:55:09.

Thursday social ride is a fixed skeleton slot ONLY through Phase 2 (until
~mid-October) — it pauses for the cold/dark season and rejoins the skeleton
whenever the athlete says it's back on.

## Phase 1 — Divca sharpening (2026-08-24 .. 2026-09-28, 5 wks)
Intent: race-specific sharpening on a fit, load-optimized base. Hold ~40–48
run-km weeks (hours cap binds — race specificity beats volume); quality = fortress stair repeats + one Fruška Gora trail long
run/week (access ride budgeted); controlled downhill doses for the ITB.
Weeks 1–3 build, then a 2-week pre-race block whose final 10 days are the
true taper. Strength stays stage 0 (no new stimulus
before a goal race). Weekly hours envelope: 8.5–9.
Key sessions: FG trail long run (Sat), stair-repeat quality (Tue), Thu ride.
The taper meso (2026-09-14 .. 09-27) carries the written Divca race-
execution rule per coach-rules periodization.

## Phase 2 — Recovery + transition (2026-09-29 .. 2026-10-11, 2 wks)
Easy running, bike, strength continuity. Hours 5–7. Last Thursday rides of
the season.

## Phase 3 — Autumn base (2026-10-12 .. 2026-12-27, ~11 wks)
The engine room for the 130k: run volume rebuilds gradually toward a
55–60 km/wk ceiling (9h cap binds), strength progresses to stage 1 (heavier
improvised load), descent tolerance grows slowly and separately, weekend
hikes maintained. Thursday becomes a run/strength slot. 3–4 wk mesocycles,
each with a down week. Hours 8–9.

## Phase 4 — Winter durability (2026-12-28 .. 2027-02-14, ~7 wks)
Back-to-back weekend long runs begin (Sat long + Sun medium/hike). Biggest
steady weeks of the year. Strength to stage 2 (hip stability under fatigue).
Hours 8.5–9.

## Phase 5 — FG 130k specific (2027-02-15 .. 2027-04-07, ~7.5 wks)
Vert-focused b2b weekends on Fruška Gora, one planned "big day" (~50–60 km or
long day-hike combo), nutrition and night-start rehearsal. Downhill volume
peaks here — gated by log/itb.md. Hours 9 (big-day week absorbed by the
following down week).

## Phase 6 — Taper (2027-04-08 .. 2027-04-27, 3 wks) → FG 130k on 2027-04-28
Taper meso must carry the written race-execution rule (pacing cap, descent
strategy, numbered fueling/hydration plan per coach-rules).

## Phase 7 — Recovery (2027-04-29 .. 2027-05-19, ~3 wks)
Bike-heavy, running by feel. Hours 4–6.

## Phase 8 — 100-mile specific (2027-05-20 .. 2027-07-04, ~7 wks)
Heat adaptation (native to the home-city summer), hiking economy with poles, b2b
big weekends, one trip weekend as the season's biggest block. Firms up fully
once the race is chosen (until then: profile 100 mi / 9,000 m+, mid-summer).
Hours 9.
