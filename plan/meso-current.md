# Mesocycle 2 — Divca taper (2 weeks) → Divca Trail, Saturday 2026-09-26

**Entry state (W37 review, 2026-09-13):** Bukovački 23.57 km / 860↑ 845↓
raced in 1:57:40 at HR 168 — an excellent fitness read 14 days out, but the
HR-cap and walk-the-descents rehearsal did not happen. Knee: eight
consecutive ≤1/10 readings since 09-04, including 0 in-race and ≤1 at 24 h
after the largest, fastest descent dose of the year. Load ratio peaked 1.33
(gate tripped → W38 is a down week by rule; the taper already is one).
Exit criteria of meso 1 met: no symptom >2/10, last long run ≥2:30 trail
time equivalent (1:57 raced at 168 bpm exceeds the stimulus). **Taper opens
normally, in descent-hold mode:** planned weekly descent ≤ 902 m, no
progression, strength stage 1 load-only.

**Block focus:** arrive at Divčibare on 09-26 fresh, sharp and with the
knee at ≤1/10. Fitness is banked; nothing done in these 13 days adds to it,
and several things could subtract from it. Week 1 (~71 % of W37 run km,
~7.4 h) keeps one short threshold touch and one easy Fruška Gora trail run
that doubles as the walk-the-steep-pitches rehearsal. Week 2 (race week) is
~15 km of easy running with strides, two maintenance strength sets, no
descent, rest Friday, race Saturday.

**What progresses:** nothing. **What holds:** strength stage 1 loads (aim
11–12 reps at the current load; race week = 1 set, no jumps), easy-run HR
≤135, threshold band 166–174 (one short touch W38, strides only W39),
Thursday ride (W38 easy-spin version because Friday is the long run; W39
optional and short).

**What is deliberately NOT tested before the race:** descent at speed. The
knee has now taken 845 m at 3:41–4:28/km with no in-race symptom, but Divca
is 1,453 m, +72 % on that, over ~3 h. That gap is closed on race day by the
execution rule below, not by a training session in the taper.

**Gate for the whole block:** any knee reading ≥2/10 → taper week 1 goes
run-free until two consecutive ≤1 readings; a ≥2/10 reading in race week →
Friday's cold reading decides whether the race is run with the walk-every-
descent rule (≤2) or not started (≥3 / lateral pain on walking).

## RACE-EXECUTION RULE — Divca Trail, Sat 2026-09-26, 28 km / 1,453 m+

Benchmark: **2025 — 27.97 km, 2:55:09, HR 153 avg, 6:16/km.** Goal ≤ 2:55.
Reference: Bukovački 09-12 — 23.6 km / 860 m at HR 168 in 1:57:40. Divca
needs ~50 % more climbing over ~50 % more time; the effort that won
Bukovački is not the effort that finishes Divca at 2:55.

**1. Pacing cap (HR is the governor, pace is a readout).**
- Start in the middle of the field, not the front. First 10 min: HR ≤155,
  whatever the field does.
- Climbs: **HR ≤165 sustained, 170 hard cap** — if it reads 170, hike until
  it reads 162, then run again. Hiking a 15 %+ pitch at a strong stride is
  not slower than running it at 170 bpm three hours out from the finish.
- Flats and runnable descents: HR 150–160.
- Target race average **HR 156–162** (2025: 153 at 2:55). Anything above
  165 average before halfway is a 2:55 given away in the last hour.
- Last 5 km: cap lifted, run what is there.

**2. Descent strategy.**
- Every descent is RUN unless one of these says walk: (a) technical/rocky
  footing, (b) pitch steeper than roughly 20 % (you cannot hold the
  cadence cue), (c) any lateral tightness at the knee.
- Cues on every descent: **cadence +5 % (target ≥180 spm), short and
  slightly wider steps, land soft under the hips, no braking strides.**
  Bukovački's 227 spm peak was braking-free but 3:41/km on 845 m; at Divca
  the ceiling on descents is **4:30/km** — faster than that buys seconds and
  spends the knee.
- First two descents deliberately conservative (4:45–5:00/km): they are the
  warm-up for the eccentric load and the best early read on the knee.
- **Stop-and-walk rule stays in force:** first lateral tightness → walk it
  out for 5 min. Not settled → walk every remaining descent; the time goal
  is dropped, the finish is not. Sharp lateral pain on walking → stop at
  the next aid station.

**3. Fueling & hydration plan (numbered, ~3 h, ~45 g carbs/h, all tested).**
Carry 7 items, eat 6 on the clock, the 7th is the spare. Start the clock at
the gun; eat at the top of a climb or on a flat, never mid-descent.
1. 0:30 — SiS isotonic gel (25 g), no water needed.
2. 1:00 — Alesto fruit bar (19.2 g) + 2–3 sips of water.
3. 1:30 — Balans cereal bar (29.5 g) + water.
4. 2:00 — SiS isotonic gel (25 g).
5. 2:30 — Alesto fruit bar (19.2 g) + water.
6. 2:45–3:00 — Haribo 30 g portion (15.4 g), a few at a time on the last climb.
7. Spare: SiS gel — use it if anything is dropped, refused, or the day runs
   past 3:10.
Total ≈ 133 g carbs over ~3 h ≈ 44 g/h. Nothing from aid stations except
water and (if familiar) cola in the last hour.
- **Fluids:** 2 × 500 ml soft flasks, one with **1 electrolyte tab**, one
  plain. ~500 ml/h at 15–24 °C; refill both at the mid-race aid station
  (check the aid-station list the evening before and name the refill point
  in the race-week plan). Drink on every walked pitch.
- **Breakfast:** 3 h before the start, ~100 g carbs (bread/jam/banana),
  familiar; nothing new. 500 ml water with an electrolyte tab in the hour
  before the start.
- **After:** 25–40 g protein + carbs within the hour. Knee reading that
  evening and the next morning → log/itb.md.

**4. Abort/adjust ladder (decided now, not on the trail).**
- Knee ≤1 through the race → plan as written.
- Lateral tightness → walk 5 min → settled: resume, descents at 5:00/km
  ceiling; not settled: walk every descent, keep climbing by HR.
- Sharp lateral pain while walking → stop at the next aid station. The
  season's real goal is 130 km in April and 100 miles in summer; Divca is
  a checkpoint.

**Exit criteria to meso 3 (Phase 2 recovery, 2026-09-27 .. 10-11):** race
completed; knee readings on 09-26 evening, 09-27 and 09-28 logged. Any
reading ≥2/10 → the recovery block is descent-free and run-light (bike +
strength) and the Phase-3 entry gate (≥2 consecutive symptom-free weeks
before a specific block) is checked at the 10-11 review.
