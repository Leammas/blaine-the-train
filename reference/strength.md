# Strength Routine — Versioned Stages

Constraint: home-based, bodyweight + improvised weight (1.5 L bottles,
backpack, band). Gym may only ever be RECOMMENDED by the coach if progression
is genuinely impossible without it; user accepts or declines. Never scheduled
by default. Frequency: 2×/week, non-negotiable.

## Stage 0 (the athlete's own routine, recorded verbatim — fallback stage)

Two sessions/week: session A at 15–16 reps/set, session B at 12 reps/set.
Warm-up: same exercises without added weight, 10 reps.

Then 2 sets of:
1. Lying hip abduction, band at knees, then 30 s hold
2. Single-leg glute bridge, then 20 s hold
3. Single-leg squats with 1.5 L bottle
4. Single-leg jump-ups, rear foot on sofa, with 1.5 L bottle
5. Single-leg straight-knee calf jumps, 20 reps, with 1.5 L bottle
6. Single-leg bent-knee forward-back jumps ~50 cm, 20 reps, with 1.5 L bottle
7. Archer push-ups, 10 reps

Plus 1 set × 20 reps each:
1. Medium step ahead, swinging bottle left–right, arms straight ahead
2. Medium step ahead, swinging bottle left–right, arms ~60° raised
3. Large step ahead, swinging bottle up–down, arms straight ahead

## Stage 1 (current from 2026-09-07 — LOAD is the single progressed variable)

Accepted by the athlete 2026-09-06. Same exercises, same order, same
warm-up (unloaded, 10 reps). Reps come DOWN, load goes UP on the non-impact
exercises; the three jump exercises are UNCHANGED (ITB rule: ≤1/10 permits
load progression, blocks plyometric/impact additions). Session A 12–13
reps/set, session B 10 reps/set.

2 sets of:
1. Lying hip abduction — heavier band or two bands at the knees, then 30 s hold
2. Single-leg glute bridge — 1.5 L bottle (or loaded backpack) on the hips, then 20 s hold
3. Single-leg squats — **backpack ~5–6 kg** (2 × 1.5 L bottles + books) instead of one bottle
4. Single-leg jump-ups, rear foot on sofa, 1.5 L bottle — UNCHANGED
5. Single-leg straight-knee calf jumps, 20 reps, 1.5 L bottle — UNCHANGED
6. Single-leg bent-knee forward-back jumps ~50 cm, 20 reps, 1.5 L bottle — UNCHANGED
7. Archer push-ups, 10 reps — unchanged

Plus 1 set × 20 reps each, with **two 1.5 L bottles (one per hand) or one
2 L bottle** instead of one 1.5 L:
1. Medium step ahead, swinging bottle left–right, arms straight ahead
2. Medium step ahead, swinging bottle left–right, arms ~60° raised
3. Large step ahead, swinging bottle up–down, arms straight ahead

Rules for stage 1: if an exercise cannot be completed with control at the
new load, drop the load back for that exercise only and note it. Any ITB
reading ≥2/10 in the last 7 days → run the session at stage-0 loads/reps.
Next variable (stage 2, not before the Divca taper ends): range, then
instability, then fatigue-state placement.

## Progression principles (for the coach when authoring stage 1+)

- Progress ONE variable at a time: load (heavier backpack/bottles), then range,
  then instability, then fatigue-state placement (strength after easy runs).
- Direction: heavier single-leg loading and hip stability under fatigue.
- A new stage is proposed in a weekly plan, appended here as "Stage N" with
  full exercise list, and only becomes current when the user confirms.
- ITB gate (updated 2026-09-05): any symptom ≥ 2/10 in the last 7 days → stay
  on current stage. Readings ≤ 1/10 and non-escalating allow load progression
  but block plyometric/impact additions (see coach-rules.md, ITB management).
